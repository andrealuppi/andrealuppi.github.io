abstract: 'Battiston et al. provide a comprehensive overview of how investigations of complex systems should take into account interactions between more than two elements, which can be modelled by hypergraphs and studied via topological data analysis. Following a separate line of enquiry, a broad literature has developed information-theoretic tools to characterize high-order interdependencies from observed data. While these could seem to be competing approaches aiming to address the same question, in this correspondence we clarify that this is not the case, and that a complete account of higher-order phenomena needs to embrace both.'
